# Shipping rates and delivery times (United States)

This article covers orders shipped to addresses in the United States. For Canada
and Mexico, see "International shipping".

## When your order ships
Orders placed before 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time on a business day ship the same day.
Orders placed after 1:00 p.m. Eastern Time, or on a weekend or US federal holiday,
ship the next business day.

## Shipping methods and costs
Delivery times are counted in business days after the order ships.

| Method | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 4-7 business days | $6.95, or free on orders of $75 or more |
| Expedited | 2-3 business days | $14.95 |
| Overnight | 1 business day | $29.95 |

The free-shipping threshold uses the order total after discounts and before tax.
For Overnight delivery, place the order before 11:00 a.m. Eastern Time.

## Alaska, Hawaii, and US territories
Orders to Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, Guam, and the US Virgin Islands ship by
Standard only. Delivery takes 7-12 business days, and a $10 surcharge is added to
the Standard rate. Free shipping does not apply to these destinations, whatever
the order total. Expedited and Overnight are not available.

## PO boxes and military addresses
We ship to PO boxes and APO/FPO/DPO addresses by Standard only.

## Oversized items
Kayaks, stand-up paddleboards, and canoes are oversized items. Each one adds a $25
oversize fee, and an order containing an oversized item is not eligible for free
shipping. Oversized items ship only to the contiguous United States.

## Tracking your order
When your order ships, we email you a tracking link. If you have not received it
within 24 hours of placing your order, check your spam folder, then contact us at
support@larchwick.example.
