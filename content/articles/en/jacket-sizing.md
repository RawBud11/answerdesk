# Jacket sizing guide

This guide applies to Larchwick-brand jackets. For other brands, use the size
chart on the product page.

## How to measure
- **Chest:** measure around the fullest part of your chest, under your arms, with
  the tape level.
- **Sleeve:** with your arm relaxed at your side, measure from the center back of
  your neck, over the shoulder, to your wrist.

## Size chart
| Size | Chest (in) | Chest (cm) | Sleeve (in) |
|---|---|---|---|
| XS | 32-34 | 81-86 | 31 |
| S | 35-37 | 89-94 | 32 |
| M | 38-40 | 97-102 | 33 |
| L | 41-43 | 104-109 | 34 |
| XL | 44-46 | 112-117 | 35 |
| XXL | 47-49 | 119-124 | 36 |

## Choosing between sizes
If your chest measurement falls between two sizes, choose the larger size if you
plan to wear layers underneath, and the smaller size for a closer fit.

## Shell jackets
Rain shells and wind shells have a relaxed fit with room for a fleece underneath.
Choose your size from the chart.

## Insulated jackets
Down and synthetic insulated jackets have a close fit to hold in warmth. If you
plan to wear a fleece or another mid-layer underneath, choose one size larger than
the chart shows.

## Still not sure?
You can exchange an unused jacket for another size. See "Returns and exchanges".
