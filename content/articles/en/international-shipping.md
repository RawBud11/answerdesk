# International shipping (Canada and Mexico)

We currently ship outside the United States to Canada and Mexico only. We do not
ship to any other country. All prices are in US dollars.

## Rates and delivery times
Delivery times are counted in business days after the order ships.

| Destination | Delivery time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Canada | 6-10 business days | $19.95, or free on orders of $150 or more |
| Mexico | 8-14 business days | $24.95 (no free-shipping threshold) |

Expedited and Overnight shipping are not available for international orders.

## Duties and taxes
- **Canada:** duties and taxes are calculated and paid at checkout, so nothing is
  due on delivery.
- **Mexico:** duties and taxes are not included in your order total. The carrier
  collects them from the recipient on delivery. If the package is refused, it is
  returned to us and refunded minus the original shipping cost.

## Items we cannot ship internationally
Oversized items (kayaks, stand-up paddleboards, and canoes) and fuel canisters
cannot be shipped to Canada or Mexico.

## Returns from Canada and Mexico
International orders can be returned under the same time limits as US orders, but
we do not provide a prepaid return label. You pay the cost of return shipping.
See "Returns and exchanges" for the conditions.
