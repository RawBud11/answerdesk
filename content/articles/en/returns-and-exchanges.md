# Returns and exchanges

## Return window
You can return or exchange items within 60 days of the delivery date. Items must
be unused, in their original condition, and with the original tags attached.

For orders delivered between November 15 and December 31, the return window is
extended to January 31 of the following year, or 60 days from delivery, whichever
is later.

If an item is defective or wears out early, it is covered by our warranty rather
than by this policy. See "Warranty".

## Items that cannot be returned
- Items marked **Final Sale**, including clearance items, cannot be returned or
  exchanged.
- Gift cards cannot be returned.
- Opened fuel canisters cannot be returned, for safety reasons.

## Exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different size or color of the same product. If
the new item has a different price, we refund or charge the difference to your
original payment method. Exchanges within the United States come with a free
prepaid return label.

## Refunds
For refunds of US orders, we email you a prepaid return label. A $7.50 label fee
is deducted from your refund. You may use your own carrier instead and pay the
shipping yourself, in which case no fee is deducted.

Once your return arrives at our warehouse, we inspect it and issue the refund
within 5 business days. Refunds go to the original payment method. Original
shipping charges are not refunded unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the
wrong item.

For returns from Canada and Mexico, see "International shipping".

## Gifts
Items bought as gifts can be returned for store credit by the gift recipient,
using the order number on the packing slip. The purchaser is not notified.

## How to start a return
Sign in, go to **Order history**, choose the order, and select **Start a return**.
If you checked out as a guest, email support@larchwick.example with your order
number and the email address used for the order.
